​DeDust has implemented a TON Bridge, enabling users to seamlessly transfer tokens between Ethereum and TON Blockchain

DeDust users can now directly transfer tokens between TON Blockchain and the Ethereum blockchain using the official bridge in the backend. Additionally, DeDust's built-in bridge includes support for the Wallet Connect protocol, enabling the use of various crypto wallets such as Uniswap Wallet, SafePal, Rainbow, and others.

Previously, in order to access TON Ecosystem from Ethereum, users had to rely on Metamask. However, thanks to these new developments, accessing TON has become easier than ever before. DeDust has also initiated a user reward program, distributing 120,000 TON tokens to liquidity providers. It is important to note that when utilizing the bridge for transfers, network fees on both blockchains still apply. Likewise, some users may experience stability issues with Trust Wallet when connecting via the Wallet Connect protocol. In this case, we recommend using the built-in Trust Wallet browser or an alternative wallet altogether.

​Introducing TON-based DAO voting solution TON.vote

During the recent TON Ecosystem referendum matters such as voting on optimizing tokenomics or implementing a network fee burn mechanism were on the agenda, with the TON.vote service playing a crucial role. Today, we would like to delve deeper into TON.vote and take a closer look at how it works.

TON.vote is a decentralized on-chain governance service specifically developed by the Orbs platform for DAOs. This protocol supports referendums and voting directly on the blockchain, eliminating the need for centralized services.

With each new vote initiated by a user, a separate smart contract is created. These contracts operate independently of the TON and Orbs teams, guaranteeing complete decentralization and allowing any user to follow the link and verify voting results.

Prominent dApps such as STON.fi, EvAA, TON Punks and Fanzee have joined Orbs as partners for the launch of TON.vote. As the protocol progresses, any service within TON Ecosystem will have the opportunity to use TON.vote for conducting fully decentralized on-chain referendums.

We hope that TON.vote will empower the ecosystem and provide a transparent platform for community decision-making.
