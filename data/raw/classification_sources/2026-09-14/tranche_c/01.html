How it works

Build an agent. Submit. Win HBAR

  1. 01

    Pick a bounty

    A new bounty opens every Monday. Pick one bounty (only one bounty entry allowed per person or team) that fits what you want to build, and check the requirements before you start.

  2. 02

    Build with the Hedera Agent Kit

    Ship your project as a public GitHub repo using the Hedera Agent Kit (JS or Python). Plugins, MCPs, and other AI Studio tooling are fair game.

  3. 03

    Submit before Sunday 23:59 UTC

    Drop your repo, a live demo or social-media post, and a short description into the submission form. Make sure you have left the required feedback on a Hedera tool.

  4. 04

    Get paid in HBAR

    Winners are announced at the end of the judging period. Payouts are in HBAR, calculated using the running average price of HBAR over the 30 days prior to the winners being announced.

Rules & resources

Everything you need to ship

  1. 01

    You can only submit a project or agent for one of the bounties. One winner will be chosen for each bounty. Any duplicate submissions will be automatically disqualified from consideration. Each Submission will be judged solely on what you and your Hackathon team has worked on during the duration of the Hackathon

  2. 02

    Agents should not pose any danger to users in terms of financial risk or liability. Agents must be implemented such that it is impossible to use or drain a user's funds without their explicit consent. Mainnet agents must use a human-in-the-loop flow, or equivalent safeguards against misuse of funds.

  3. 03

    All submissions are automatically considered for the bonus bounty.

  4. 04

    The payout for all bounties is paid in HBAR, calculated using the running average price of HBAR over the 30 days prior to the winners being announced. Bounties will be paid up to 90 days following the final bounty deadline.

  5. 05

    See the complete Rules for all requirements and information about the bounties and their prizes. Submission of an agent to this bounty program means that you agree to our Terms & Conditions.
