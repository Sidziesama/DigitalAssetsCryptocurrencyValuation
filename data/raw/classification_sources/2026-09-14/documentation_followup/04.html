At the request of @valardragon and @aquietinvestor we are updating the CDRGP question options as follows:

(1) Accept

(2) Reject — Do Not Support the Proposed Project

(3) Reject — Would Reconsider in a Future Round at a Lower Amount

(4) Abstain

Both (2) and (3) are “no” votes and will be tallied as such. The purpose of (3) is to signal to applicants that they are on the right track but coinholders believe that the requested $ amount is too high for the work completed. To be clear: there is no obligation or expectation that coinholders will vote to accept a request that has been rejected due to (3) given as a response.

Feedback welcome. Thanks!
