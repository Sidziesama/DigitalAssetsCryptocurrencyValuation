# Cryptoasset advisory profiles

These profiles translate the verified economic-function matrix into a practical advisory starting point. They do not assign investment scores, price targets, or recommendations.

## BTC

**Architecture context:** PoW

**Economic identity:** money or store-of-value use; collateral and financial integration

**Potential value channels:** money or store-of-value use; collateral and financial integration

**Key cautions:**
- No verified direct route from protocol value to the token or holder.

**Closest economic comparables:** ETH (collateral and financial integration, money or store-of-value use), LTC (money or store-of-value use), BCH (money or store-of-value use)

**Advisory action:** Compare the asset with its economic peers, verify that each mechanism is live on the decision date, and assess market, custody, liquidity, legal, and concentration risks separately.
