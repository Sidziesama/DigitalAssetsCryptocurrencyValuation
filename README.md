# DigitalAssetsCryptocurrencyValuation

### Purpose 
Document readings on existing research in valuation for Digital Asset and Cryptocurrency Advisory.   

### Research Motivation  
Digital assets have evolved from speculative instruments into an emerging asset class that includes cryptocurrencies, stable coins, tokenized real-world assets (RWAs), decentralized finance (DeFi) protocols, NFTs, and utility tokens. Despite increasing institutional adoption, there is still no universally accepted framework for valuing these assets or incorporating them into traditional portfolio management. Unlike equities or bonds, most digital assets do not generate predictable cash flows, making classical valuation techniques such as discounted cash flow (DCF) or dividend discount models inapplicable. Consequently, investors often rely on market sentiment, technical analysis, or simple network metrics. This research seeks to develop a systematic framework for evaluating digital assets by integrating traditional financial factor models with blockchain-native indicators. The ultimate objective is to build a Digital Asset Advisory framework capable of estimating expected returns, measuring risk, explaining price movements, and supporting portfolio allocation decisions.  

### Objectives  
- Objective 0: Shortlist Potential Problem Statements that comprehend research expectations
- Objective 1: Construct a Taxonomy of Digital Assets: Investment Focus
- Objective 2: Develop Valuation Frameworks for each asset category.
- Objective 3: Build Factor Models Explaining Returns
- Objective 4: Model Return Distributions
- Objective 5: Develop an Advisory framework   

### Research Questions 
- What fundamental factors determine the value of different categories of digital assets?
- Can traditional financial factor models explain cryptocurrency returns?
- Which blockchain-specific variables improve explanatory power beyond traditional models?
- Can digital assets be classified into economically meaningful categories with different valuation methodologies?
- Can a factor-based advisory framework generate superior investment recommendations?
